Technical approach

Why no CMS?

WordPress powers 43% of the web, but it's also the number one target for cyberattacks. For a small business showcase site, a CMS is overkill: constant updates, plugins that break, slow load times, security holes.

A static site loads in under a second, never goes down and cannot be hacked. There's nothing to maintain. It's the most reliable and fastest option for a professional showcase website.

The result: a Google PageSpeed score above 95/100 and a site that just works.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a professional website cost?

A professional showcase website starts at €500 with OptimyCloud. This price includes up to 5 pages, a responsive design, SEO optimization, a contact form, Google Analytics and the first year of hosting. It's a one-time payment, not a monthly subscription.

What is the delivery time for a showcase website?

A showcase website is delivered within 48h of receiving your content. All you have to do is fill in a form with your details (name, business, services, service area, photos, contact details) and I take care of the rest. No pointless meetings, no drawn-out process.

Why a static site rather than WordPress?

A static site loads in under a second, never goes down, needs no security updates and cannot be hacked. WordPress adds needless complexity for a small business or tradesperson's showcase site. To learn more, read our article Do you really need a CMS?

Is hosting included in the price?

Yes, the first year of hosting and the domain name are included in the €500 price. After that, hosting is €5 per month. It's a static site, so there's no expensive server to maintain. No commitment, you can stop whenever you like.
