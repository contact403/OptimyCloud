Frequently asked questions

How much does WordPress troubleshooting cost?

WordPress troubleshooting starts at €250 excl. VAT. This covers the diagnosis, fixing the issue and a detailed report of the work done. The final price depends on the complexity of the problem identified during the diagnosis.

How quickly do you fix a WordPress issue?

Work starts within 24h of getting in touch. In most cases, the diagnosis is made in under 5 minutes and the fix follows straight away. For complex cases (hacked site, corrupted database), allow 24 to 48h.

My WordPress site shows a 500 error: what should I do?

A WordPress 500 error can be caused by a faulty plugin, a corrupted .htaccess file, an exceeded PHP memory limit or a server issue. The first step is to check the error logs. Send us your access details, we diagnose and fix it within 24h. For a detailed guide, see our article on diagnosing WordPress 500 errors.

My WordPress site shows a white screen, how do I fix it?

The WordPress white screen (White Screen of Death) is often caused by a plugin conflict, a faulty theme or a PHP memory overflow. The diagnosis involves enabling debug mode, identifying the source of the conflict and fixing it. It's a routine issue that we resolve within a few hours.

My WordPress site has been hacked, what should I do?

If your WordPress site has been hacked, you need to act fast: identify the infected files, clean up the malicious code, change all passwords, update WordPress and the plugins, and harden security to prevent it happening again. We step in within 24h with a complete report of the actions taken.
