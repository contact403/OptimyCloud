Frequently asked questions

Why no CMS like WordPress?

A static site loads in under a second, never goes down, needs no updates and can't be hacked. WordPress is great for a blog. For a tradesperson's website, it's needless complexity.

How does the AI chatbot work?

The chatbot is trained on your business information: your services, your service area, your indicative pricing. When a visitor asks a question, it answers instantly and collects their contact details for you. You get a notification by email or WhatsApp.

Can I edit my site after delivery?

One round of revisions is included in the price. Later changes (adding a page, editing text, new photos) are billed on request. No hidden subscription.

What do I need to get started?

You fill in a form with your details (name, business, service area, services, photos, contact info). I handle the rest. No completed form, no site launched. It avoids the back-and-forth.

Is hosting included?

The first year is free. After that, hosting is €5/month. It's a static site, so there's no costly server to maintain.
