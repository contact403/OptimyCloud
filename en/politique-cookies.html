Cookie Policy

1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small text file stored on your computer, tablet or smartphone when you visit our website. Cookies allow us to recognise your device and to remember certain information about your preferences or past actions.

2. How do we use cookies?

OptimyCloud uses cookies to:

  • Improve the operation of our website
  • Analyse the use of our site
  • Personalise your experience
  • Remember your cookie preferences
  • Measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns

3. Types of cookies used

3.1 Strictly necessary cookies

Purpose: These cookies are essential for the website to function.

Examples:

  • cookieConsent: Remembers your choice regarding cookies
  • Session cookies: Keep your session active

Legal basis: Legitimate interests (operation of the site)

Duration: Session or 1 year

✓ Enabled by default (necessary)

3.2 Analytics and performance cookies

Purpose: These cookies help us understand how you use our site.

Service: Google Analytics

Data collected:

  • Pages visited
  • Duration of visit
  • Traffic source
  • Actions performed on the site

Legal basis: Consent

Duration: 25 months maximum

⚙️ Requires your consent

3.3 Advertising cookies

Purpose: These cookies allow us to measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns.

Service: Google Ads

Use:

  • Conversion tracking
  • Advertising ROI measurement
  • Improved targeting

Legal basis: Consent

Duration: Variable (30 days to 2 years)

⚙️ Requires your consent

3.4 Third-party service cookies

Calendly: For appointment management

Formspree: For form management

GitHub Pages: For hosting

Legal basis: Consent or legitimate interests

Duration: Variable depending on the service

⚙️ Depending on the services used

4. Managing your preferences

Cookie settings

You can change your cookie preferences at any time:

Other management methods:

Via your browser:

  • Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies
  • Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies
  • Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions

Opt-out tools:

  • Google Analytics: Opt-out extension
  • Google Ads: Ad settings
  • YourOnlineChoices: Management platform

5. Consequences of refusing cookies

Strictly necessary cookies: Refusal may prevent the site from functioning properly.

Analytics cookies: Refusal does not affect your browsing, but prevents us from improving our site.

Advertising cookies: Refusal does not affect your browsing, but may result in less relevant advertisements being shown to you.

6. Cookies and personal data

Some cookies may contain personal data. The processing of this data is described in our Privacy Policy.

You have the same rights over this data (access, rectification, erasure, etc.).
