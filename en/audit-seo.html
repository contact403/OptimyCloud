Organic-search audit

What the SEO audit covers

Every factor that influences your Google ranking is examined in detail. Nothing is left to chance.

Core Web Vitals

LCP, INP, CLS: the performance metrics Google uses to rank your site. Analysis and recommendations for each indicator.

Schema markup

A review of your structured data (JSON-LD) for Google rich snippets: FAQ, reviews, products, breadcrumb, organization.

Indexing and crawl

Robots.txt, XML sitemap, orphan pages, redirects, 404 errors. We make sure Google can crawl and index every one of your pages.

Content and keywords

Title tags, meta descriptions, Hn structure, duplicate content, keyword cannibalization. Every page is analyzed individually.

Mobile experience

Google indexes the mobile version first. Responsive design, button sizes, readability, viewport: it all gets checked.

Security and HTTPS

SSL certificate, mixed content, security headers, HTTP-to-HTTPS redirects. An insecure site is penalized by Google.

Common issues

What's holding you back on Google

Here are the problems we run into most often during an SEO audit. If any of these sound familiar, your site needs a diagnostic.

Pages not indexed by Google

A misconfigured robots.txt, forgotten noindex tags, a missing sitemap. Google can't rank what it can't see.

Load times that are far too long

Uncompressed images, render-blocking JavaScript, a slow server. A slow site drives visitors away, and Google knows it.

Duplicate or thin content

Pages with little text, identical titles, missing descriptions. Google favors unique, in-depth content.

Technical errors (404, 500, redirects)

Broken links, redirect chains, 500 server errors. These problems degrade the user experience and your ranking.

No structured data

Without schema markup, Google can't display rich snippets (FAQ, reviews, prices). You lose visibility in the search results.

Site not mobile-friendly

Google uses mobile-first indexing. A site that doesn't display properly on a smartphone loses positions.

Frequently asked questions about the SEO audit

What is an SEO audit and why do one?

An SEO audit is a complete analysis of your website to identify everything holding back your visibility on Google. It covers the technical side (speed, indexing, security), the content (keywords, structure, tags) and authority (links, popularity). It is the essential first step before any organic-search strategy.

How much does an SEO audit cost?

The price of an SEO audit depends on the size of the site, the number of pages and the technical complexity. At OptimyCloud, every audit is quoted individually to fit your situation. Expect anywhere from a few hundred to several thousand euros depending on the scope. A free initial call lets us assess your needs.

How long does a complete SEO audit take?

A complete SEO audit generally takes between 5 and 10 business days depending on the size of the site. This includes the technical analysis, the content audit, the indexing review and writing the report with prioritized recommendations.

What results can I expect after an SEO audit?

After an SEO audit, you receive a detailed report listing the issues found, ranked by priority and by impact on your Google rankings. Sites that apply the recommendations see, on average, an improvement in their rankings within the following 3 to 6 months. The gains depend on the competition and on how the plan is implemented.

Does the SEO audit include fixing the issues?

The SEO audit includes the analysis, the report and the detailed action plan. The fixes can be handled by your own team by following the report, or entrusted to OptimyCloud as part of a separate engagement. Every recommendation is documented with the technical instructions needed to implement it.
